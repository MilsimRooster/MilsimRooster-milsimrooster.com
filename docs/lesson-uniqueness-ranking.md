# Lesson Uniqueness Ranking

## Scoring Method

This score is a template-detection score, not a theological grade.

Each lesson was checked for 8 template signals:

1. Repeated summary sentence.
2. Repeated Understand It sentence.
3. Repeated teen explanation pattern.
4. Repeated life application template.
5. Generic repeated quiz distractors.
6. Repeated prayer template.
7. Repeated activity template.
8. Repeated discussion-question template.

Uniqueness score:

- Starts at 100.
- Loses points for each template signal.
- Gains a small amount for vocabulary variety.
- Starter lessons receive a small non-template bonus because they avoid the generated-library pattern.
- Capped at 100.

Engagement score:

- 30 possible points.
- Six 1-5 categories: curiosity, storytelling, emotional impact, memorability, replay value, classroom usefulness.
- In the table, `C/S/E/M/R/Class` means curiosity/storytelling/emotional/memorability/replay/classroom.

Important: The numbers are useful for ranking repetition. They should not be treated as a final content-quality rubric.

## Ranking Interpretation

The ranking proves a split, not a smooth curve.

| Group | Lesson count | Signal count range | Uniqueness range | Engagement range |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Starter lessons | 3 | 0 | 100 | 28/30 |
| Generated-library lessons | 74 | 8 | 19-21 | 13-14/30 |

The 74 generated-library lessons are not equally bad in biblical concept. Some cover important passages. But by the measured template criteria, they are equally templated.

## Final Ranking Conclusion

The claim "74 of 77 lessons show generated-template quality" is supported.

The ranking found exactly 74 lessons with all 8 template signals and exactly 3 lessons with zero template signals.

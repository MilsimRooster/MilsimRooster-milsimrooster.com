# Bible Study Arcade Playtest Notes

Use this for church-family and small-group feedback before launch.

## What To Watch

- Which game gets the most "one more round" reactions?
- Does Parables Quiz now feel deep enough for repeated rounds?
- Which questions feel too easy, too obscure, or worded weird?
- Do wrong answers teach without sounding preachy?
- Do adults and youth both understand the explanations?
- Does the page feel fast and simple on phones?
- Are any Scripture references questionable or worth rewording?

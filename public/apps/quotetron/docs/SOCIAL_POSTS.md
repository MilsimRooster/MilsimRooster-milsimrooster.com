# Social Posts

## Twitter/X

New free tool: Quotetron.

Enter your quote, hidden hours, expenses, fees, and minimum hourly target. If you do not know what to charge, use the rate helper first. It tells you whether to take the job, negotiate, or pass.

No account. No database. Runs in your browser.

https://milsimrooster.com/apps/quotetron/

## Facebook

Freelancers, laborers, and side-hustlers: before you say yes to a project, run the quote through Quotetron.

It helps you pick a starting rate, checks your real hourly rate after admin time, revisions, expenses, platform fees, and cash set-asides, then gives a simple take / negotiate / pass signal.

https://milsimrooster.com/apps/quotetron/

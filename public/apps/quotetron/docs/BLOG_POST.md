# Introducing Quotetron

Quotetron is a lightweight Milsim Rooster utility built to help working people build better quotes without making visitors wait.

## Why It Exists

Freelance jobs can look profitable until admin time, revisions, payment fees, materials, and cash set-asides are included. This tool turns those assumptions into one clear number: the quote needed to clear your minimum hourly target.

## How To Use It

Enter the project quote, estimated work hours, client/admin hours, revision buffer, out-of-pocket expenses, fee percentage, cash reserve percentage, and minimum hourly target. The calculator updates instantly with the true hourly rate, target quote, net after costs, total hours, and a take / negotiate / pass recommendation.

If you do not know what hourly rate to start with, use the rate helper. Pick the closest task type, choose your experience level and market, then apply the typical hourly rate or suggested total quote to the calculator.

## What Comes Next

The first version is static and complete. Future additions can stay lightweight: a downloadable worksheet, examples for common freelance services, or a short pricing guide linked from the result card.
